01 — Chapter Studio

Cut spatial stories like a film editor.

On the Mac, Chapter Studio gives you a full-width, multi-track timeline for time itself: every reveal, move, fade, video and sound in your scene is a clip you can drag, trim and retime.

  • Derived clips, not busywork. Clips are a live projection of your actions — reveal-to-hide, play-to-stop. Drag a clip and the underlying actions retime with it.
  • Drag-to-add action library. Eighteen categorized action chips — Show, Fade, Move, Scale, Rotate, Play Video, Spark Burst and more — drop right onto tracks and snap to step boundaries.
  • Non-destructive video trims. Slip in-points, set out-points, or consolidate to clip length — masters are never re-encoded until you say so.
  • Scrub like a pro. Click any step and the RealityKit viewport shows exactly what's on-stage at that moment — playback running or not.

02 — Chapter Vision

Step inside and sculpt the scene with your hands.

On Vision Pro, your chapter isn't a preview — it's the editor. Look at an entity, pinch, and move it. Grab a gizmo ring to rotate on a world axis. Keyframe a pose from where you're standing. The room is the canvas.

immersive editing — gaze + pinch
  • Gaze-pickable everything. Entities, gizmo arms, timeline clips — look, pinch, drag. A procedural gizmo with world-axis arms, rotation rings and a uniform-scale cube.
  • A full studio in one window. Session rail, asset & action libraries, spatial timeline, animation graph dock, and inspectors — floating wherever you want them.
  • Import from where you are. Pull media straight from Photos or Files on the headset. It lands in the scene instantly and uploads to the Mac in the background.
  • Solo mode. No Mac around? Create, open and save full .chapterscript bundles entirely on-device — with undo, redo, and recents.
03 — Animation

Ten channels. Bézier-native. One truth.

Keyframes live on the segment — position, rotation and scale on every axis, plus opacity — with real tangent handles, per-key interpolation, and continuous Euler rotation that never flips. The same evaluator drives the Mac, the headset, and playback. What you author is what plays.

  • A real graph editor. Channel chips, draggable keys with axis lock and frame-grid snap, unified or broken tangents, pinch zoom, double-tap to insert — on Mac and Vision Pro alike.
  • Auto-key that respects you. Recording only re-keys channels you've already animated. A virgin channel is never keyed by accident.
  • Motion trails in the room. On Vision Pro, animated entities draw their path through space — grab an orb on the trail and reshape the move where it happens.
  • Rotation done right. Continuous Euler degrees with explicit rotate order and live filtering — wind up three full turns if the story calls for it.

04 — Live Sync

Save on the Mac. Blink on the headset.

An embedded Bonjour server publishes your chapter on the local network. Every ⌘S pushes to the headset in under a second — and edits flow the other way too. Move an entity on Vision Pro and it lands in the Mac's undo stack as one clean entry.

05 — Particles

Weather, magic, and atmosphere on tap.

A full particle design surface — emission shapes from spheres to toruses, physics with mass and drag, evolving color ramps, vortex and attraction force fields, even emitters that spawn emitters. Tune every parameter with a live preview that never restarts the simulation under you.

particle preset — ember_rise
  • Nine emitter shapes — point, sphere, box, plane, cone, cylinder, torus and more, with birth locations on the surface, volume or vertices.
  • Evolving color — constant, random, or two-color evolution with power curves; opacity curves from gentle fades to hard pops.
  • Force fields — noise turbulence, point attraction, and vortex swirl, composable per preset.
  • Burst or loop — one-shot bursts fire when an entity is revealed; loops run for ambience. Secondary spawned emitters ride along for fireworks-grade effects.
06 — And the rest

Everything a spatial storyteller actually needs.

Immersive backdrops

Wrap a segment in a 360° or 180° video sphere — mono, side-by-side, over-under or MV-HEVC — or a full USDZ environment.

Interaction gates

Hold a step until the audience taps or a timeout passes — with an optional on-screen prompt.

Passthrough or portal

Per-segment presentation: fully immersive, mixed with the real room visible, or a plain window. Switch mid-story.

Primitives, lights & text

Drop in spheres, boxes, planes, 3D text and lights straight from the add menu — no external modeling required to block a scene.

Asset folders

Organize scene assets and backdrops into folders, duplicate and re-target with a right-click, and keep big projects navigable.

Deep undo, everywhere

Every timeline drag, inspector tweak and spatial edit is one clean undo entry — on the Mac and on the headset.

Photos & Files import

Local-first media on Vision Pro: picks land in the scene instantly, hash-matched and background-uploaded to the Mac with retry.

Steps that hold curves

Playback gates pause the animation clock, not the story — curves hold their pose and resume exactly where they left off.

Typed forms + raw JSON

Every action has a typed parameter form — and a raw JSON escape hatch when you want to hand-tune the long tail.

07 — Open format

Your story is a readable file, not a black box.

Chapters save as ChapterScript — an open, documented directory bundle: one pretty-printed chapter.json plus your assets, manifested with SHA-256 hashes. Diff it, version it, generate it, play it anywhere the format is implemented.

chapter.json
{
  "formatVersion": 2,
  "experienceID": "the-lighthouse",
  "displayName": "The Lighthouse",
  "defaultSegmentId": "arrival",
  "segments": [{
    "id": "arrival",
    "presentation": "immersive",
    "immersiveBackdrop": {
      "video": "shore_360.mov",
      "field": "deg360" },
    "steps": [{
      "duration": 8,
      "gate": { "type": "tap" },
      "actions": [
        { "revealEntity": { "name": "lantern",
          "fadeIn": 1.5 } } ] }],
    "animationTracks": [ /* bézier keys */ ]
  }]
}
  • One bundle, everything in it. chapter.json + an assets/ folder. Copy it, zip it, ship it.
  • Sorted-keys, pretty-printed. Saves are deterministic, so version control diffs show exactly what changed in your story.
  • Integrity built in. Every asset is manifested with byte size and SHA-256 — players cache aggressively and never show a stale frame.
  • Made to outlive the tools. Forward-compatible migration on open; tolerant decoding on every field added since v1.
